# Lemmings, since 1991

Pull up a chair. There's a level to finish.

One player has the controls. Everyone has a plan. Ultimate Lemmings brings
that shared, slightly chaotic joy to a new roomful of players.

Pause, try a route and pass the controls. The old puzzles still have plenty
to teach us.

## The original creators

DMA Design created Lemmings. Psygnosis published it.

The original Amiga credits name:

- Programming: Dave Jones
- Animation: Gary Timmons
- Graphics: Scott Johnston
- Music: Brian Johnston and Tim Wright

## A game across many machines

Presage, Inc. brought Lemmings to the Macintosh, with its own detailed colour
and black-and-white artwork. Tony Williams arranged the original music for DOS.
Other ports, composers and arrangers have their own identities in the soundtrack
catalogue.

## Preserved and recreated

Classic's DOS, Amiga and Macintosh artwork comes from the original releases.
The Macintosh black-and-white option uses the original separate artwork banks.

The optional Macintosh-style artwork for The Tribes, Chronicles and supported
NeoLemmix levels is a reconstruction made for Ultimate Lemmings. It refines the
original pixels while keeping the level geometry and game rules.

Original versions, composer recordings and remixes retain separate soundtrack
credits. Community format research, replay archives and fan levels continue the
work of preserving these games.

Ultimate Lemmings is an unofficial native port by Erik Veland. The original games,
artwork and music retain their creators' ownership.
