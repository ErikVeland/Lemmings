Ultimate Lemmix

Erik Veland developed Ultimate Lemmix. Community level makers created the packs you can explore, collect and play in your own order.

The little crowd brings the urgency. The planning remains yours.

Lix artwork and fonts

Lix supplies the selected CC0 character sprites, menu icons and scenery. See the Lix media notice for creator credits. The DejaVu Sans Condensed Bold font has its own font licences.

The game interface uses the shared pixel lettering. Imported original fonts depend on the source files you supply.

Original game heritage

DMA Design created Lemmings, published by Psygnosis. The original credits name Dave Jones for programming, Gary Timmons for animation, Scott Johnston for graphics, and Brian Johnston and Tim Wright for music.

Presage, Inc. produced the Macintosh port. Tony Williams arranged the original DOS music.

These credits acknowledge the works supported by imported data. They do not imply that the Store app includes those original assets.
