Common questions

Why does a game say “No data”?

The Store app includes three starter puzzles. Other campaigns need compatible game files. A visible import control does not mean those files are included.

Why does Original still show Lix characters?

Original presentation needs its source artwork. The independent starter levels always use Lix artwork, even when Original is selected for restored campaigns.

Why does my import lack music or interface artwork?

A raw game folder can supply only the assets it contains. A DOS folder cannot also supply Macintosh interface artwork or absent soundtracks.

Why are my hints general advice?

Checked example routes require an exact match with the installed level and engine data. Other levels receive labelled general coaching.

Why is progress different on another device?

Check the selected player, solo or Hot Seat session, and cloud-library connection. Imported media and mid-level engine checkpoints are not included in the cloud library.

Keep the original device and its saves while investigating a sync problem. Do not remove the app as a first troubleshooting step.

Can I play without a separate account?

Local play does not require a hosted account. Cloud saves, Game Center and community rankings have separate service requirements.

Where can I download the full restoration collection?

No public restoration download is currently listed. You can import compatible data from your own collection.

Report a problem

  1. Note the app version and build shown in the app.
  2. Include your device model and operating system version.
  3. Name the game, campaign and level.
  4. Describe what happened and the steps that preceded it.
  5. If useful, attach a screenshot without private information.

For an import problem, describe the source format and quote the error. Do not send complete copyrighted game collections without first arranging a suitable transfer.
