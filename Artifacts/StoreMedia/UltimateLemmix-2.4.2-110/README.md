## Artwork and capture sources

The bridge and umbrella illustrations use the build 110 Lix mascot as their visual reference. Image generation produced the woodland scenes and the separate Floater figure.

The search film follows the illustration with Floater frames from the bundled *Float to Safety* puzzle. The game advances at 17 ticks per second. The film follows the falling characters over the illustrated woodland background.

The screenshots use Mac and simulator captures from build 110. The Mac Nuke image combines the in-game Metal effect with its matching interface capture. The Apple TV captures use the production game and Hot Seat views in a capture host.

The [source archive](Source-Masters.zip) contains the unframed captures, original artwork, build 110 app icon and wordmark. It also includes the animation layers, game frames and notices. The [file manifest](manifest.json) records dimensions and checksums for the 17 delivery files.
