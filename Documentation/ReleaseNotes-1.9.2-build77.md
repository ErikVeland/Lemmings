# Ultimate Lemmings 1.9.2 — Celebrations, rewards and smoother navigation

Build: 77
Release base: v1.9.1

This maintenance update restores completion celebrations and precision rewards,
and improves navigation during play and in Records.

## Celebrations and rewards

Securing the rescue goal restores the celebratory music transition. A subtle
golden glow adds to the moment and honours reduced-motion and reduced-flash
settings. Classic, Lemmings 2 and Lemmings 3 share the completion treatment.

Learning and Hot Seat journey results now credit earned Zooms and Superzooms.
Results show the newly awarded amounts. The existing rules remain: one Zoom per
three unassisted stars and one Superzoom per three distinct unassisted three-star
levels. Assisted runs and repeat clears do not create extra rewards.

## Learning journey

Cellbash is now lesson 2. Come back! moves to lesson 71 in Intermediate.
Existing journeys retain their current level, history and players, including
unvisited lessons moved earlier in the sequence.

## Navigation and music

The gameplay Menu button fades after a few seconds and returns on hover or
keyboard focus. Moving to the top edge releases cursor capture so the Mac menu
bar remains accessible. Capture stays released while a menu is open.

Records supports Previous, Next and arrow keys through the current campaign,
including levels without a saved run. A song change briefly shows its title and
soundtrack in pixel lettering, then fades without blocking gameplay clicks.

The shared artwork module keeps Mac controls consistent with the iOS beta work.
Settings include contextual help, and compatible shared leaderboard records can
open action replays. iOS 2.0 remains a separate device beta and is not part of
these macOS downloads.

## Compatibility and validation scope

Universal macOS app for Apple silicon and Intel, macOS 12.3 or later. Both full
and slim downloads retain original Macintosh music. Automatic updates carry the
full music library, with a signed delta from build 76.

Lemmings 2: The Tribes remains Complete. Lemmings 3: Chronicles and NeoLemmix
remain Beta. The existing original-engine, campaign and ending limits remain.

Checks cover shared engines, preserved route manifests, journey migration,
reward accounting, muted music transitions, Records input, notices and rendered
pixel controls. Native UI tests run offscreen with all test audio muted.
Audible listening, foreground launch, physical Intel and minimum-macOS checks
remain open. Final package and publication evidence is recorded separately.
