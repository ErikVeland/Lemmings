# Ultimate Lemmix 2.7.5

Build: 119
Release base: e21a86c0
Platform builds: Mac 119, iPhone/iPad/Duo 116, Apple TV 116

On iPhone and iPad, the app shows Home before checking restored files. Longer
level loads show an intro without delaying play. Pause to Assign starts off
unless you previously enabled it. Settings now has an About page.

After a win, returning to Main Menu saves your place in a Journey or playlist.
Starter playlists offer short routes through compatible levels.

Choose Catalan or Swiss German in the language picker. Menus and Home artwork
make it easier to find your next puzzle.
