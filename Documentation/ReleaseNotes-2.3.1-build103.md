# Ultimate Lemmings 2.3.1

Build: 103
Release base: 96dc81b8491dfdb58683f0e32025fc4c8137aa1c
Platform builds: Mac 103, iPhone/iPad/Duo 38, Apple TV 17

## Find your way back

Home keeps Start Journey or Resume Journey ready for the selected solo or Hot Seat session. Earlier saved Hot Seats remain available alongside the current one. Returning to a level restores its saved place, and clicking a level preview opens that level.

## Controls that stay together

Skills and transport controls use joined stone groups with clearer selected and pressed states. Back and Main menu sit with the other actions at the bottom of Mac panels. Menus and level browsers keep their layout steady as artwork loads.

## Rewind and busy scenes

Classic takes shorter rewind steps through recent play. Larger levels refresh the area you are viewing as you move around. Pause, selection and replay controls remain available while the scene is busy.

## Music and sound

Moving between screens keeps the current tune ready to resume. Repeated start requests no longer restart its fade-in, and sound effects have a steadier path during busy play.
