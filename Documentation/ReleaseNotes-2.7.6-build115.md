# Ultimate Lemmings 2.7.6

Build: 115
Release base: 3bc976e9d9cf0ea94ff650330a5cfa1c71a5a1c7
Platform builds: Mac 115, iPhone/iPad/Duo 48, Apple TV 23

Supported controllers add tactile feedback for bridge warnings, steel strikes,
fatal falls and explosions. The nuke has distinct arming, fuse and blast cues.
Feedback follows the action on screen and stops when you pause, rewind or leave
a level. Haptics can be changed independently of the sound settings.

Game-data downloads show waiting and alternate-server states, with specific
messages for connection problems and insufficient storage.

About brings release notes, credits and a community forum link together, with
bundled information available offline.

The release runs on macOS 12.3 or later on Apple silicon and Intel.
