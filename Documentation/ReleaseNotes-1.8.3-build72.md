# Ultimate Lemmings 1.8.3 — Worldwide Records

Build: 72
Release base: v1.8.1

Changes since the public 1.8.1 release, including the local 1.8.2 test builds.

## Worldwide and local records

Worldwide speed, rescue and career leaderboards now work without Game Center.
Sharing is optional for each local player, under their initials. Game Center
remains a separate option in supported builds. Watch available recorded runs
from the hosted boards, or remove your shared records and movies.

Fastest clear and Fastest 100% have separate leaderboards. Rewind-assisted runs
are kept separate. The result screen shows your best clear time, and local record
movies are retained for playback. Hosted scores are community submissions, not
independently verified solutions. Movie uploads have storage and size limits.

## A better learning journey

The 294-level journey includes the Macintosh level Going their separate ways and All the 6s in
their intended positions. Cellbash moves earlier. Covox and Something wrong are
replaced with more suitable lessons. Fan levels in the default journey are
bundled for offline play. Existing journey history and player ownership carry over.

Continuation into unopened fan packs, saved-run resume, pack-to-pack navigation,
tall NeoLemmix level scrolling and Escape behaviour have been corrected.

## Rescue goals and anonymous totals

Checked completion solutions provide best-known rescue targets for three-star
awards and philosophy assessments. Levels with a checked winning solution no
longer leave the final star unknown. Best-known records remain distinct from
proven maximum rescues.

Optional anonymous rescue totals use the hosted aggregate counter. These totals
remain separate from player identities, public scores and rescue proofs.

## Sound and presentation

Solution replays include sound effects. Rewind effects play backwards with a
limited mix to prevent repeated rewinds becoming excessively loud. Beast of a
level has a better-balanced Modern mix. Hot Seat handovers and level changes
avoid brief unwanted tracks and use smoother music transitions.

The Macintosh snowman artwork now matches its collision shape. What's New stays
within a scrollable window. Additional L3 level-specific creature corrections
are included.

## Compatibility

Universal macOS app for Apple silicon and Intel, macOS 12.3 or later.
NeoLemmix remains Beta. Lemmings 2 and Lemmings 3 remain Preview.
The slim download includes essential music and offers optional soundtrack
libraries. Automatic updates retain the full soundtrack bundle.
