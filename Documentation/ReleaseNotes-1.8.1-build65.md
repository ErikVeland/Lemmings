# Ultimate Lemmings 1.8.1 — The Full Journey

Build: 65
Release base: v1.8

This update covers the whole 1.8 line: 1.8 and 1.8.1.

## The full journey

Oh My! All Lemmings! returns to 292 curated levels, official and fan, in a
Redux-anchored order. The first seven teaching levels stay in order. Old
built-in journeys migrate with their history, ownership and progress. Custom
playlists remain unchanged.

## Explosions stay on the terrain

Classic, NeoLemmix, Lemmings 2 and Lemmings 3 keep each explosion at its world
position. Camera pan, zoom, window resize, CRT curvature and the precision lens
move the effect with the terrain.

## Sharper pixels and sound (1.8)

Pixel artwork keeps crisp edges across game views, controls, level previews,
hints and original movies. NeoLemmix plays hatch, assignment, rescue, death and
nuke sound effects through the selected Classic sound bank. After the final nuke
explosion, slowed music returns to normal speed in Classic, NeoLemmix and
Lemmings 2.

## NeoLemmix controls and camera

The - and + keys now do the same as the on-screen buttons. A double tap on - or
+ goes to the fastest or slowest rate. Each level opens with the camera centred
on the first hatch, in both directions.

## Lemmings 3

Brick pickups give eight uses. Hatch order, Bricks, Shimmy ceiling wear and
Mole wall patrols follow the original game more closely. Lemmings 2 and 3
remain Preview. Their full completion milestone is 1.9. NeoLemmix remains Beta.
Mobile remains the separate 2.0 milestone.
