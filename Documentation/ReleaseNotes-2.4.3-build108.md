## Skill feedback and rewind

A rejected skill shows the reason. In The Tribes on Mac, a rejected
assignment keeps the forward history while you rewind.

## Chronicles controls

Selecting a skill in Chronicles shows the original pressed artwork.

## Short games

Classic follow-up adds five levels from the original DOS game, Fun 6–10.
On Mac, open Playlists, then Short games. On iPhone, iPad, Duo and Apple TV,
open Collections, then Short games.

## Compatibility

macOS 12.3 or later, on Apple silicon and Intel. Classic and The Tribes are
Complete. Chronicles and NeoLemmix remain Beta.
