# Ultimate Lemmings — beta 12

Version 0.1, build 12. Universal Mac app for Intel and Apple silicon, macOS 13 or later.

## Changes since beta 11

- Oh Yes! More Lemmings now loads artwork from the correct source for each rank.
  The Sunsoft conversion retains its own ground metadata and special background.
  All 60 levels pass rendering and lemming-release checks. This is not evidence
  that every converted level has a winning solution.
- NeoLemmix levels that require unsupported skills or objects are rejected with
  an explanation before the current playfield is replaced. Zero-stock unsupported
  skills do not block an otherwise compatible level.
- Lemmings 3 plays its bundled tribe module music. Music follows volume and mute
  settings, suspends for replay playback, and is included in exported recordings.
- Lemmings 3 now plays six named original voices for the entrance, assignments,
  rescues, deaths and bomb activation. Effects follow mute and volume settings
  and are included in replay recordings.
- The Lemmings 3 pause menu now offers **Original movies**. Watch all five
  bundled movies, pause with Space, and return with Escape. These gallery movies
  currently play without a soundtrack.
- The campaign gate now checks 88 winning replays across Oh No!, Xmas and
  Holiday, plus 16 Lemmings 3 levels. It detects missing or changed recorded
  evidence and checks each recorded input in a fresh simulation.
- Fast-forward ghosts are much fainter and shorter. They follow actual movement,
  including slopes and diagonals, while the solid lemmings remain sharp and draw
  above every ghost. The directional blur is cached.
- Release checks now include every Oh Yes! level and explicit rejection of
  unsupported NeoLemmix mechanics. The stale Classic replay checklist is corrected.

## Tester focus

1. Upgrade from beta 11 and check profiles, progress, records and achievements.
2. Switch between all three Oh Yes! ranks, including the final Sunsoft level.
   Check terrain, objects, entrances, exits and artwork switching.
3. Play Lemmings 3 in each tribe. Check music, mute, volume, replay audio,
   sleep/wake and returning to the library without overlapping music. Check
   assignment and rescue voices, and pause and close each original movie.
4. Try compatible and incompatible NeoLemmix levels. A rejected level must name
   the missing mechanics and must not replace the current playfield.
5. Play crowded scenes at normal and fast speed in flat and CRT modes. Check
   slopes, falling lemmings, high-resolution displays and longer sessions.

Record the game, rank and level, Mac model, macOS version, display mode and exact
steps for any failure. Preserve a movie or input replay where available.

## Remaining limits

This beta is a release-candidate evaluation, not a claim that every campaign is
complete. The original DOS campaign has 120 verified winning replays. Lemmings 2
has 64 recorded level completions. Lemmings 3 has 16 fixed-input winning replays
and still uses provisional mechanics in several areas. Environmental effects,
original movie soundtracks and story triggers remain unconnected. The extra
classic-family campaigns have 88 winning replays; only Xmas 1991 has full coverage.

NeoLemmix compatibility remains partial. Rewind is not available in both sequels.
Records remain local. A demonstrated rescue count is not an upper-bound proof.
This package remains for private beta testing under the project's game-data policy.
