# Ultimate Lemmings 2.4

Build: 104
Release base: 96dc81b8491dfdb58683f0e32025fc4c8137aa1c
Platform builds: Mac 104, iPhone/iPad/Duo 39, Apple TV 18

## Find your way back

Home offers Start Journey or Resume Journey for the selected solo or Hot Seat session. Earlier saved Hot Seats remain available, and level previews can open the selected level.

## Clearer controls

Joined stone controls group skills, speed and transport actions. Selected lemmings and skills are easier to see, and a pulsing direction marker shows which way a lemming is walking. Mac panels keep Back and Main menu in the bottom action row. Duo layouts use more of the screen, while menus keep their position as artwork loads.

## Rewind and busy scenes

Classic keeps recent rewind states close for shorter steps. Repeated rewind presses on Apple TV can move further back. Scene rendering and input handling have been reworked to reduce unnecessary work during play.

## Music and sound

Music transitions and sound-effect processing have been updated to reduce interruptions during busy play and when moving between screens.

## Progress on another device

Cloud Saves offers a private, one-use device code without a new account. Linking keeps existing players and combines their progress, including saved Hot Seat campaigns. Mid-level checkpoints stay on the device where you played.

## Compatibility

Classic and The Tribes are Complete. Chronicles and NeoLemmix remain Beta. The mobile Journey is Classic-only.
