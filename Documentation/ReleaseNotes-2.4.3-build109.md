# Ultimate Lemmings 2.4.3

Build: 109
Release base: 55f8f52703e04db6b6f9460fd7b962de81aab26f
Platform builds: Mac 109, iPhone/iPad/Duo 47, Apple TV 22

## Mac playability

Intel Macs start with speed trails and the full-screen fast-forward effect off.
The game enables them when both the CPU and GPU have spare capacity. When
frames arrive late, the game reduces optional effects automatically.
Classic and NeoLemmix also do less work when you click a lemming in a crowd.

## First launch

New Mac players can choose a profile and start the first level from the same
screen. Returning players keep their existing profile.

## Skill feedback and rewind

A rejected skill shows the reason. In The Tribes on Mac, a rejected
assignment keeps the forward history while you rewind.

## Chronicles controls

Selecting a skill in Chronicles shows the original pressed artwork.

## Short games

Classic follow-up adds five levels from the original DOS game, Fun 6–10.
On Mac, open Playlists, then Short games. On iPhone, iPad, Duo and Apple TV,
open Collections, then Short games.

## Compatibility

macOS 12.3 or later, on Apple silicon and Intel. Classic and The Tribes are
Complete. Chronicles and NeoLemmix remain Beta.
