# Ultimate Lemmings, beta 7

This is the first build to reach testers since beta 1. It lists everything that
changed between the two.

## Read this first

Beta 1 made macOS warn you before it opened. We signed that build, but we never
sent it to Apple. Apple has now notarized this build, so it opens like any other
app.

1. Download the zip file.
2. Double-click it to unpack.
3. Move `Ultimate Lemmings.app` to your Applications folder.
4. Open it.

If macOS still warns you, tell us. It means something is wrong on our side.

## New name and icon

The app is now `Ultimate Lemmings`. It has its own icon.

## Gameplay fixes

- Lemmings walk further into an exit before they drop in. They used to
  disappear when only half of the body was over the hole.
- The cursor no longer sticks to a lemming. It used to grab the nearest one
  within about ten pixels, which made two lemmings side by side hard to tell
  apart. Now it picks the one you point at.
- A sound plays when you give a lemming a job. The game used to stay silent.
- Hold the plus or minus button to run the release rate up or down. One press
  used to move it one step.
- Zoom on the menus no longer moves the background sideways.

## Screen settings

The monitor and television settings drew nothing at all in beta 1. They work
now.

- Menus always draw sharp. Only the game itself goes through the tube.
- The flat panel setting works. It used to leave an empty window.
- The game remembers your choice between runs.
- The tube settings are gentler than before. Tell us if they are still too
  strong or now too weak. Use Tube Strength to adjust.

## Sound

- Amiga sound effects are available. Choose them under Audio.
- The modern sound setting sounded muffled. It cut the middle of the sound, added
  reverb, narrowed the stereo, and turned the volume down. It now has more
  weight and more attack.
- The modern setting also moves drums toward the middle. The Amiga put each
  channel hard left or hard right, so a drum sat in one ear.
- Tell us if an instrument that is not a drum moved to the middle. Name the
  track.

## New levels

- `Oh Yes! More Lemmings!` holds 60 levels that never reached the DOS release.
  Twenty come from the Amiga two-player set, ten from Oh No! More Lemmings, and
  thirty from the Mega Drive version.
- `Fan Levels` opens 6035 levels from 535 fan-made packs. Choose a pack, then
  choose a level. `Play All` runs the whole pack in order. `Shuffle` runs it in
  a random order.
- Fan levels remember which ones you passed. A star marks each one you finished.
- The front screen counts your progress through the fan levels.

To use the fan levels, point the app at the folder of packs one time.

## Removed

Four entries left the game library. They were SNES Sunsoft Special, Genesis
Presenter, Arcade Extra Levels, and Amiga 2-Player. Three of them never had any
levels behind the name. The fourth moved into `Oh Yes! More Lemmings!`.

## Download size

The soundtrack is now Apple Lossless instead of WAV. The quality is the same.
The download is about 100 MB smaller.

## Known problems

- Twenty-three fan packs use a special background picture. Those levels draw
  without it.
- Lemmings 2 runs under the classic rules only.
- Lemmings 3 is a preview.
- We can read the level lists off the SNES and Mega Drive cartridges. We cannot
  play those levels yet. Only the Mega Drive levels somebody converted will run.

## What to tell us

Report the game and the level where a problem happens. Name the artwork, music
and sound settings in use. Say whether the problem returns after a restart.
