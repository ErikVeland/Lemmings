# Ultimate Lemmings, beta 9

Version 0.1, build 9. Requires macOS 13 or later on Intel or Apple silicon.
This beta adds Macintosh-style sequel artwork, nuke undo controls and brighter
explosion cores on compatible displays. It includes the beta 8 changes.

## Lemmings 2 and 3 artwork

- Macintosh-style 2× artwork is now the default for both sequels. Lemmings 2
  menus, introduction screens, briefings and controls use it too.
- The artwork refines terrain textures, character colours, liquid highlights
  and small metal details while preserving the original gameplay geometry.
- Choose **Options → Graphics → Lemmings 2 + 3 → Macintosh-style 2× artwork**
  to switch styles. Lemmings 2 Preferences and the Lemmings 3 artwork button
  share the same saved choice.
- Switching artwork during play preserves the camera, selected skill and game state.
- Fixed walkers that kept their PC appearance in Beach and other tribes despite
  the artwork setting. All tribes now show the Macintosh-style face, eye,
  cuffs and shoes while retaining their own hair colours.
- This is a reconstruction based on Macintosh reference artwork, not an
  official Macintosh sequel asset set.

## Controls and explosions

- Double-click the nuke button to activate it in the classic player and Lemmings 2.
- While the nuke is active, click the button again to restore the game to its
  state before the nuke, including terrain and timers. A double-click also undoes it.
- In the classic player, press **X** to activate or undo the nuke.
- Classic bomb bursts flash briefly, then disappear without shrinking or fading.
- On displays with available extended dynamic range, explosion cores can reach
  up to four times standard white brightness in flat and CRT modes.
- Terrain and controls retain standard brightness. The extra brightness expires
  while paused. Standard displays retain the ordinary flash.

## Lemmings 3 fix

- Empty cells in object overlays no longer erase the collision properties of
  solid terrain beneath them.

## Known limits

- Lemmings 2 includes all twelve tribes and 120 campaign levels. Full campaign
  walkthrough coverage and original-engine equivalence remain unverified.
- Lemmings 3 remains a preview without connected music, sound effects or movie playback.
- NeoLemmix files have no gameplay sound effects or general rewind. Fencer and
  laserer remain unsupported.
- Some fan-pack special background pictures remain unavailable.
- Native SNES and Mega Drive cartridge campaigns are not playable.

Unpack the zip, move **Ultimate Lemmings.app** to Applications, and open it.
No extra game files are needed for the bundled campaigns.

Report the game, level, display mode, artwork and audio settings used.
