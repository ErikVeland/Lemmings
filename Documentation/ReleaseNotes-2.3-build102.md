# Ultimate Lemmings 2.3

Build: 102
Release base: v2.2.2
Platform builds: Mac 102, iPhone/iPad/Duo 36, Apple TV 15

## Easier to find your way back

The shared menus give each platform a clearer route through the game. Home,
Back, favourites, recent levels and saved playlists keep their place across
Classic, Lemmings 2, Lemmings 3 and supported NeoLemmix play. Fan collections
are easier to browse, and numbered packs stay together. Pause and replay pages
keep their main actions in reach while longer lists scroll.

On Mac, keyboard and controller routes reach the same actions. On iPhone, iPad,
Duo and Apple TV, touch, focus and remote layouts adapt to the screen while
keeping shared choices and saved identities intact.

## More ways to play a short session

Golden Path progress now credits a won lesson to its original collection.
Lemmings 2 adds an isolated practice route and a short Classic tribe sampler
that carries the right survivors between levels. Level skips show their
available balance and preserve the starting population; a failed handover
returns the skip.

## Native play across the three games

Mobile and Apple TV connect more of the native Lemmings 2 and Lemmings 3
controls, campaign sessions, checkpoints and replay tools. Lemmings 2 supports
its machine input and held aiming controls. Sequel attempts can be reviewed
from recorded actions, and original Lemmings 2 and Lemmings 3 movies can be
opened from the shared library while play stays paused.

Supported NeoLemmix levels retain finite extra-skill charges through recovery.
Across the games, crowd shadows, assignment feedback and optional sprite trails
make busy scenes easier to read. TV keeps its performance limits.

## Progress follows the player

Cloud sync now requests a final save pass when the app closes or moves to the
background. It waits for active uploads, then checks again for changes made
while an upload was running. Offline play and local mid-level checkpoints keep
their existing behaviour.

A Hot Seat keeps one session for its players. Starting the Golden Path on Mac
continues the current Hot Seat. Offline saves of the same session from two
devices now merge. Earlier builds started the Golden Path in a second session
with the same players. The first sync merges that session's Classic progress
into the Journey session. Every device then continues the Journey session.

## A steadier soundtrack and clearer controls

Repeated startup and audio-engine requests no longer restart a live recorded
soundtrack's fade-in. Shared pause and replay controls use clearer groups and
distinct states, with layouts for compact phones, Duo screens, tablets, Mac and
TV. The Desk Clock keeps its pose-specific entry and pause behaviour.

## Duo home and audio stability

Duo Home fits on both screens and when folded. Its controls sit over the moving
home scenery, without a dark panel behind them. The portrait menu stays below the
camera cutout and above the home indicator. The layout keeps its position when
loading status appears. Menu audio waits through brief fold transitions
and lets launch settle before the first theme. Audio playback also rebuilds its
output after media-service resets. The main menu logo stays optically centred
when status text or corner controls appear.

## Player rankings

Progress and Leaderboards have separate destinations. Local and hosted rankings
show one ordered list across the available categories. Level results include
player rankings for Fastest 100%, Fastest clear, Least skills and Most saved.

## Platforms and scope

Version 2.3 covers Mac, iPhone, iPad, Duo and Apple TV. Mac ships first; iOS,
tvOS and sideloading channels follow their own delivery checks. The existing
Classic and The Tribes Complete classifications remain unchanged. Chronicles
and NeoLemmix remain Beta, with their recorded route and source limits. Human
language review and physical-device acceptance remain separate from source and
render checks.
