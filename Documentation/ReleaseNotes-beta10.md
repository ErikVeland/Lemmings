# Ultimate Lemmings — beta 10

Version 0.1, build 10. Universal Mac app for Intel and Apple silicon, macOS 13 or later.

## Changes since beta 9

- Fan Levels opens directly, with 535 embedded packs and 6,043 readable levels.
  Each launch checks the Lemmings Level Database for new compatible packs and
  downloads additions in the background. The collection remains available offline.
- New Lemmings 2 campaigns start at Beach. Existing tribe selections and progress
  remain intact. Briefings identify the tribe beside the level number.
- L2 intro, menus, map, briefings, results and endings have finer Macintosh-style
  2× artwork and lettering. Level previews use their tribe's terrain treatment.
- Results, records, profiles, settings and replays stay inside the game window.
  Macintosh bitmap fonts, stone borders and clearer priorities replace the separate
  records window. Successful runs lead to the next level; retry challenges remain optional.
- Local arcade profiles have initials, sprite portraits and separate campaign progress.
  Each level tracks rescue records, skill use, achievements and retry targets.
- THE TROLLEY adds optional rescue stars and attempt analysis. One star meets the
  native goal; two reward an extra rescue; three require the verified maximum or
  a supported full rescue. Unknown maximums remain labelled as unknown.
- Finished runs support variable-speed replay and MP4 movie export.
- Explosions have brighter orange-and-white bursts. Optional full-screen HDR
  flashes are available in Video settings and remain off by default.
- DJ transitions wait until the rescue goal is met and prefer triumphant tracks.
  L2, L3 and other port soundtracks join the mix through an option enabled by default.
- Fast-forward adds fading sprite afterimages with additive blending and a sharp current frame.
  The final ten seconds play a warning sound each second.

## Tester focus

Check an upgrade from beta 9 and a fresh profile. Open Fan Levels without choosing
any folder, try an offline launch, and open a level from a bundled pack. Test L2's
artwork toggle on the briefing and during play. Complete and retry levels, switch
profiles, review records, and export a replay at different speeds.

Verify music transitions before and after the rescue goal. Try fast-forward,
last-second warnings and explosion effects in flat and CRT display modes.
HDR output depends on the display's available headroom.

## Scope

Records and rankings are local. Maximum-rescue claims depend on available evidence;
observed personal records are not treated as verified limits. Rescue stars never
replace native progression rules. The sequel artwork is an inferred Macintosh-style
reconstruction. Fan updates cover compatible packs in the database, not arbitrary
forum attachments or unsupported engines. Replay movies use SDR output.
