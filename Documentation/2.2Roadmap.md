23 October, Australia/Brisbane time. Status: focused Duo fixes verified; consolidated packaging and device checks pending.

The owner prioritised the device work in [the roadmap through 3.0](Roadmap.md)

## Launch scope

- Deliver to the existing iOS and tvOS TestFlight groups. Apple processing and
  beta review remain external dependencies. An upload alone is not availability.
- Preserve Classic and The Tribes Complete, and Chronicles and NeoLemmix Beta.
  Continue route and source-fidelity work separately from launch preparation.
- Keep shared controls, Retry/Continue, saved ownership and paused Ready handovers
  aligned across Classic, L2 and L3. Avoid new optional features before the candidate.
- Validate Duo through its installed Simulator and synthetic pose/render checks.
  The owner has no physical Duo before Friday. Do not claim physical Duo acceptance.
- The owner can test Mac, iPhone, iPad and Apple TV. Record their actual devices,
  OS versions and build numbers with the results.

## Candidate checklist

- [ ] Preserve the same run, selected skill and camera through closed, open,
  tabletop, rotation and resized layouts.
- [x] Cancel pending assignments, direction pickers and gestures anchored to an
  old layout. A cancelled pinch or scroll must wait for a fresh gesture.
- [x] Preserve Start/Ready when an unplayable window size returns to a usable size.
  Do not resume a running or interrupted attempt automatically.
- [x] Inspect controls, status, minimap and results around division and camera
  regions, including compact sizes. Verify input targets as well as pixels.
- [ ] Verify Solo/Hot Seat ownership, queue identity and assistance through Retry,
  Continue, Menu, relaunch and restore in all three native engines.
- [ ] Verify physical iPhone/iPad touch, controller, VoiceOver, interruptions and
  recovery. Verify Apple TV remote/controller input, focus and cache recovery.
- [ ] Confirm source/resource identity, affected regression results and unchanged
  engine evidence. Keep missing assets and skipped native checks explicit.
- [ ] Assign consistent candidate marketing versions and new platform build numbers
  after checking existing deliveries. Preserve the published stable Mac feed.
- [ ] Validate signed archives, symbols, content, entitlements and upgrade behaviour.
- [ ] Upload to the existing groups and record processing, review and availability.

Completed items above refer to the focused native Simulator and bitmap checks
in the verification record. Full system input journeys and physical acceptance
remain separate open checks. The owner has requested consolidation and publication
of the latest fixes through the active release pipeline.

## Manual device pass

Use the exact candidate and a disposable test profile. Record device, OS, build,
game, input and result for each check. Preserve existing player saves.

1. Start a fresh game, choose a skill, assign deliberately, pause and zoom.
2. Rotate or resize during a drag, pinch, held control and L3 direction choice.
   Confirm no unintended assignment or camera jump follows the transition.
3. Retry, finish or leave to Menu, then resume the same player and run.
4. Start Hot Seat, pass a turn, interrupt the app and return. Wait for Ready.
5. Quit and relaunch with a saved attempt. Confirm skill, camera, queue and owner.
6. Repeat the critical actions with the controller or remote and VoiceOver.
7. Check live progress sync between available devices, then offline recovery.
8. Play a sustained session and check input, frame pacing, heat and memory behaviour.

Repeat the native-game paths in Classic, L2 and L3. Record unsupported paths and
failures individually. For Duo, use the Simulator for pose changes and reserved
regions, with hardware touch, thermal behaviour and physical accessibility still open.
