# Ultimate Lemmix 2.7.6

Build: 120
Release base: ccf6b675d277f3b136f975503e7401d849655a92
Platform builds: Mac 120, iPhone/iPad/Duo 117, Apple TV 117

On iPhone, iPad and Apple TV, Play no longer opens a blank level when required
game data is missing. The app opens Game Data and keeps your saved Journey or
playlist intact until a level is ready.

On iPhone and iPad, the loading intro stays visible behind the startup data
check. Full visual effects return on Duo and capable iPhone and iPad models.

Game-data downloads show waiting and alternate-server states, with specific
messages for connection problems and insufficient storage. On iPhone and iPad,
downloads can continue in the background and reconnect when you return. A
stalled connection offers an alternate download, and cancelling a download
also stops a transfer that is still starting.

About adds version history and contributor pages on iPhone, iPad and Apple TV,
with the same content available on Mac. Bundled credits remain available
offline, and you can retry a failed refresh.

The Store candidate adds an optional developer tip under About, showing the
price and currency supplied by Apple. Each tip is a separately confirmed
one-off payment and unlocks no features. Availability depends on product setup
and App Review approval.

Supported devices and controllers add tactile feedback for bridge warnings,
steel strikes, fatal falls and explosions. The nuke has distinct arming, fuse
and blast cues. Haptics follow the action on screen, stop when play pauses or
rewinds, and can be changed independently of the sound settings.
