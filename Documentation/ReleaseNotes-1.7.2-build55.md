# Ultimate Lemmings 1.7.2 — Faster Controls, a Selective Journey and NeoLemmix

Build: 55
Release base: v1.7.1

These notes cover all of 1.7: the 1.7.0 release and the 1.7.1 and 1.7.2 updates.

## New since 1.7.1

- Double-tap − or + to set the release rate to the minimum or the maximum.
  A single press still changes the rate by one step.
- Press N to nuke. Press N again to undo the nuke while undo is still possible.
  Outside play, N in Classic still opens the next level.
- Oh My! All Lemmings! now selects 292 lessons: eight skill introductions,
  160 intermediate applications, 90 difficult lessons and 34 expert challenges.
  Official levels take priority within comparable difficulty bands. The corpus
  includes all 120 Classic, 100 Oh No! and 72 seasonal levels. Repeated tutorials,
  duplicate observed applications and port copies are excluded. One transition
  remains flagged for playtesting. L2/L3 mechanics stay outside this journey.
- 30 more Classic fan levels can be completed. The DOS rules ignored the exits
  on these levels. The exits now work, as they do in the fan editors.
- 2,136 Classic fan levels now have a verified win.
- A lemming that falls too far and lands in an exit is now saved, as in DOS.
- NeoLemmix: A Disarmer disables a trap when it starts work, so the lemmings
  that follow can walk past. Builders, Platformers and Stackers continue their
  work correctly after a teleporter. Stackers start at the correct height.
- Lemmings 2 and Lemmings 3 stay in Preview. This update adds more checks of
  both games against the original releases.

## Controls

- The selected lemming has a brighter halo and a small solid marker above its
  head. Reduced motion keeps the highlight steady.
- The controls help (?) shows every gameplay key, including the new keys.
- The skill panel returns to full height after you start a level from a menu.

## Oh My! All Lemmings!

- The recommended Classic path is a learning journey from Fun to Expert. It shows the
  next lesson, its stage and focus, and your solved count.
- The journey is for one player. It leaves out two-player levels, and a puzzle
  that several ports repeat appears once.
- Pause a lesson to get hints, keep a level for later, or leave. Choose
  **Revisit** to play the levels that you kept for later.
- If a session cannot start, the message names the level and pack that stopped it.

## NeoLemmix

- The game includes 788 levels from NeoLemmix Community Edition 1.2.0:
  Lemmings Redux, the NeoLemmix Introduction Pack and Original Lemmings.
  Open them from **NeoLemmix** on the home screen. No download or folder is needed.
- 713 levels are ready to play. The other 75 levels use community styles that the
  game does not include. They show as unavailable until you add a NeoLemmix folder
  that contains those styles.
- Add your own NeoLemmix packs from Level Select. Your packs appear beside the
  included packs.
- Records, previews, playlists and saved runs keep stable pack and level IDs.
  Completed levels show a completion mark.
- Lemmings use the sprite style of the level theme, in the NeoLemmix draw order.
- Pickup skills, teleporters, secondary gadget layers and moving backgrounds follow
  the NeoLemmix Community Edition timing rules.
- NeoLemmix support stays in Beta. Some levels can still behave differently from
  NeoLemmix itself.

## Sound

- The Amiga sound sets use Macintosh samples for effects that the Amiga bank
  does not have.
- Pause stops all sound effects, not only the music.

## Fixes

- Solo and Hot Seat journey sessions start correctly when macOS opens the app
  from a temporary location.
- Bundled campaigns, imported level packs, saved runs and playlists keep their
  identity after an update or when you move the app.
- NeoLemmix lemmings are no longer drawn upside down.
- The game no longer traps the pointer in a window that is not on screen.

## Downloads

- Existing players receive the full update automatically. The full archive,
  `UltimateLemmings-1.7.2-build55.zip`, is also available for a manual upgrade.
