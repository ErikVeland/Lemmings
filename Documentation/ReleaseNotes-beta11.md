# Ultimate Lemmings — beta 11

Version 0.1, build 11. Universal Mac app for Intel and Apple silicon, macOS 13 or later.

## Changes since beta 10

- Music no longer overlaps if the game is paused and unpaused in quick
  succession or the app is briefly hidden. The previous track is stopped before
  the new one starts, and a mid-crossfade suspension resumes cleanly.
- Results lead with one rescued count, the requirement, and the best possible or
  best-known rescue. Repeated statistics, rescue percentages and confusing star
  thresholds have been removed. Retry is on the left; Next level is on the right.
- Play style names open short philosopher and run explanations. The names appear
  without question marks. Rewinds use an explicit Used / Unused record filter.
- Achievements span levels. Unsupported objectives are hidden, while previously
  earned awards remain. Run details show separate statistics and omit empty
  hatch, nuke, rewind and undo counts.
- All 120 original DOS levels now have winning replays checked on the native
  engine. Of these, 103 rescue the full population. Verified maxima and demonstrated
  rescue records have distinct labels; an unknown limit stays unknown.
- Destructive skills now apply the original DOS steel-probe rules. This fixes
  routes blocked by overly restrictive terrain masks.
- Fast-forward trails use cached sprites instead of full-frame captures, keeping
  the current sprites sharp and reducing drawing work.
- Lemmings 3 uses its original panel artwork and bitmap lettering, with working
  skill, timer and playback controls.

## Tester focus

Upgrade from beta 10 and check existing profiles, progress and awards. Complete
and retry levels, switch the rewind filter, and click play style names. Check
that Next level advances and Retry restarts the same level.

Try fast-forward in all three games, including crowded scenes. Check the Lemmings 3
panel and its artwork setting. For a full-rescue check, Worra lorra lemmings can
save all 80: removing the ground beneath a blocker releases it.

## Scope

Records remain local. A winning replay proves that its rescue count is achievable;
it does not prove that further rescues are impossible. Best possible is reserved
for matching proof. The app includes the recorded soundtracks and bundled fan packs.
