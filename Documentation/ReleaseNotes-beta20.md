# Ultimate Lemmings — beta 20

Version 0.1, build 20. Universal Mac app for Intel and Apple silicon, macOS 13 or later.

## Hot Seat

- Result buttons name who will retry or continue. Both players can choose another attempt after a win or loss.
- Handovers wait for the next player to choose **Ready**. Held Return does not skip the handover.
- Classic, Lemmings 2 and Lemmings 3 show the active attempt owner's portrait and initials. Results also name that player.
- Shared campaign saves, the roster and next turn survive quitting. **Return to solo** keeps shared progress for later.
- Changing players during a level offers a saved exit to the library. Shared checkpoints cannot resume inside a solo campaign.
- **Every level** passes the turn after each result. **At first fail** lets a winner continue. Both retain explicit retry choices.

## Speed and controls

- Tap **F**, click **Speed**, or tap the controller's right trigger to toggle fast-forward.
- Hold **Shift**, **Speed**, or the right trigger for a temporary boost. Release to return to the previous speed.
- Speed arrows or **Shift+[ / Shift+]** choose 2×, 3×, 5× or 10×. The panel shows the current speed and remembered choice.
- **F**, **Escape**, or controller **B** returns boosted play to normal speed immediately. Repeated clicks do not restart a stopped boost.
- Controls help follows the active engine and preferences, including OG controls, controller support and fixed-speed mode.
- **B** cycles through matching skills. **U** selects Floater. **i** and **F1** open level hints.

## Readability and navigation

- **Settings > Accessibility > Text and menu size** offers 100%, 125% and 150%. Enlarged shared pages scroll.
- VoiceOver can navigate more individual menu and panel controls, including profile initials.
- **Tab / Shift-Tab** selects Arcade page buttons. **Return / Space** activates them.

## Please test

1. Play a Hot Seat level through a win and a loss. Check each named retry and continue action, then the Ready page.
2. Quit during shared play, reopen, and resume. Return to solo, then rejoin Hot Seat. Check that the saves remain separate.
3. Try speed toggles, held boosts, speed arrows and quick exits in all three games.
4. Switch OG/modern controls and compare controls help. Try larger menus and keyboard navigation.

## Known limits

Physical-controller behaviour and a full VoiceOver listening journey remain unverified. The accessibility additions do not provide a complete spoken representation of live terrain and lemmings.

Mid-level takeovers remain deferred until shared-attempt attribution is implemented. Campaign solution coverage and NeoLemmix compatibility remain incomplete. This is a private test beta, not a 1.0 release.

The standard archive keeps local records and has Game Center disabled. The separate Game Center archive requires a registered test Mac and cannot be notarised.
