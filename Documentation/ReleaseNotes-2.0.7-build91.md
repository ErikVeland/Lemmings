# Ultimate Lemmings 2.0.7 for Macintosh

Build: 91
Release base: v2.0.6

## Safer count reset

Clearing this Mac's play counts now asks **ARE YOU SURE?**, with Back selected.
**Restore previous counts** recovers the preceding totals and keeps rescues
earned after the reset.

## About and supporters

About now has tabs for published version history and contributor credits.
Version history works offline. Contributor credits refresh from the server,
with a saved list available when offline.

Donate is available on Home and in About. Thank you to tobiasampersand and
hays60 for supporting Ultimate Lemmings. Public credits show opted-in names
in contribution order and keep amounts private.

## Menu music

Most launches now play the Amiga title theme. The occasional alternative can
include the Macintosh theme and recordings from other versions.

## Earlier 2.0 updates

Earlier updates added Original Macintosh 1-bit artwork, resumed journeys and
playlists, Classic ghost solutions, Hot Seat ghosts, clearer worldwide level
identification, controller stepping and faster drawing, records and results.
See the About version-history tab for the published notes.

## Compatibility and scope

This release publishes the universal macOS app for Apple silicon and Intel,
with macOS 12.3 or later. The slim fresh-install download includes essential
music and offers optional soundtrack libraries. Automatic updates retain the
full soundtrack.

Classic and The Tribes retain their Complete classifications. Chronicles and
NeoLemmix remain Beta. Existing device and engine validation limits remain
documented. Release gates were approved by the release owner for this quick
patch. Engine, gameplay and launch tests were not rerun.

iPhone, iPad and Apple TV are not published by this Mac release. Their source
includes the shared Donate action and simulator effect/pointer fixes for a
later device build.
