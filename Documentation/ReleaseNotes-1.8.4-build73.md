# Ultimate Lemmings 1.8.4 — The Joy Update

Build: 73
Release base: v1.8.3

More rescue cheers, playful feedback and music that carries the celebration.

## Keep the winning tune

Meeting the rescue target can lift the current theme to an available, documented
composer recording or remix of the same tune. The Adaptive DJ keeps that theme
looping through the result screen. It keeps an existing remix, and leaves the
current version playing when no suitable upgrade is installed or alternate
soundtracks are disabled. Wins reached while paused resume the selected music.

Recorded soundtracks have measured volume trims that preserve their dynamics.
Versions with verified source loops play the intro once, then repeat the loop
without the recording's extra repeated pass or ending fade.

## Let the whole crowd cheer

Every rescue keeps its own voice, including simultaneous arrivals and crowded
exits. The chorus stays at full strength while useful assignment and warning
sounds remain clear. Player volume, mute and distance still apply.

Lemmings 2 adds rescue cheers, trampoline bounces and trap feedback. Lemmings 3
adds short sounds for tool and clock pickups, projectile launches, explosions,
brickwork, steel, water, drowning and traps. Sounds follow the actual event,
and a trap or explosion does not add a second generic death voice afterward.

## Little rewards and useful cues

An established rescue, skill or fastest-time personal best, or a newly earned
rare award, adds a small flourish to the final result note. Fastest clear and
Fastest 100% both qualify. The flourish adds no extra note or longer fanfare.

Result sounds follow live effects volume and mute, stop when the result loses
focus, and remain available with Reduced Motion. Reduced Motion reveals the
stars immediately.

Fresh starts and retries play Ready when the countdown finishes. Hot Seat
handovers wait for the next player's Ready action. Timer warnings now sound
different from builder warnings. Rejected actions on a lemming get a short,
positioned cue, and locked Classic levels use the game's sound preference.

## A cartoon speed pedal

Holding fast-forward winds the soundtrack's pitch up smoothly between speed
tiers. It settles on musical intervals, with a perfect fourth at the top.
The soundtrack's tempo and the existing simulation speed tiers stay as before.
Releasing the hold restores the previous cruising speed.

## Compatibility

Universal macOS app for Apple silicon and Intel, macOS 12.3 or later.
NeoLemmix remains Beta. Lemmings 2 and Lemmings 3 remain Preview.
The slim download includes essential music and offers optional soundtrack
libraries. Automatic updates retain the full soundtrack bundle.
