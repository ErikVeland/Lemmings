# Ultimate Lemmings 2.2

Build: 97
Release base: v2.1.2

## A little time for lemmings

Desk Clock shows the time in Classic Macintosh pixels. Lemmings rebuild the
digits each minute. When you allow calendar access, the clock can show the next
event and a countdown. Open it from the iPhone or iPad app, or add the widget
to an iPhone, iPad or Mac. The clock stays silent and does not enter a game.

On Duo, the clock can open when the app is idle in a supported pose. The app
uses available screen regions to place its controls. A layout change cancels
an unfinished touch gesture. Gameplay and Hot Seat Ready handovers remain
under player control.

## Easier to read and choose

Reading pages leave more space between paragraphs and limit the text width.
Mobile settings group related choices and show checked states. Cursor style,
paused inspection and Hot Seat ghost visibility now have explicit controls.
Mac Desk Clock widgets join the existing desktop app.

## What carries forward

Classic and The Tribes remain Complete. Chronicles and NeoLemmix remain Beta
with their documented content and engine limits. The mobile Journey still
plays Classic levels. The Desk Clock uses Classic Macintosh art.

Mac supports Apple silicon and Intel on macOS 12.3 or later. iPhone and iPad
need iOS 16 or later. Apple TV remains in the existing TestFlight channel.
Physical Duo, widget gallery, calendar permission and device acceptance need
checks on the signed builds. TestFlight processing and beta review determine
when the mobile builds become available.
