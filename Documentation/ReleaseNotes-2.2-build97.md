# Ultimate Lemmings 2.2

Build: 97
Release base: v2.1.2

The clock has stopped. The lemmings are still moving.

## Take a little time

Open the Desk Clock from Home on iPhone and iPad, or use the new desktop clock
and widgets on supported Apple devices. The clock can show your next calendar
event. Calendar access is optional, and the app cannot change your events.

On iPhone Duo, the clock can open when the app is idle on Home. It stays out of
gameplay and waits for you to return.

## Keep control through a change

Resizing or changing the Duo pose now cancels gestures that use the old layout.
The game keeps the selected skill and current run. Start and Hot Seat Ready stay
paused until you choose to continue.

Hot Seat ghosts, paused inspection and cursor settings now use shared controls
across Classic, The Tribes and Chronicles. Mobile settings have clearer groups,
and long reading pages have more space between paragraphs.

## Before you settle in

Mac build 97 supports Apple silicon and Intel on macOS 12.3 or later. iOS build
20 and tvOS build 10 use the existing TestFlight groups. Apple processing and
beta review remain separate from upload completion.

Classic and The Tribes retain Complete. Chronicles and NeoLemmix remain Beta,
with their existing content and engine limits. The mobile Journey remains
Classic-only. Mid-level checkpoints and ordinary playlist queues stay local.
