# Ultimate Lemmings 2.0.8 for Macintosh

Build: 92
Release base: v2.0.7

## Comfortable play, original character

- Basic 2× Zoom is free in Classic, The Tribes and Chronicles. It has no balance
  or failure cost. Existing earned Superzoom balances remain separate.
- Original and Modern now choose presentation. They preserve control, controller,
  interruption and assistance preferences. New profiles use restrained defaults
  and start without a required presentation-choice page.
- Help says **General coaching** and **No level-specific hints available.** when
  an exact checked hint is absent. Checked routes remain deliberate examples.
- Results keep career accounting and reward reasons in optional review. Phone
  and TV award rows stay still until selected.
- Modern assignment pointers use distinct empty, eligible and unavailable shapes
  as well as colour. Original cursor artwork remains unchanged.
- About includes offline original-team and port credits, with original and
  recreated artwork distinguished.
- Short games offers optional five-level openings with exact source checks.
  Mac supports Classic and The Tribes with isolated survivor carry-over;
  mobile/TV supports Classic. Unavailable sequel choices explain their limits.

The [2.1 roadmap](2.1Roadmap.md) now prioritises access, truthful help, newcomer
teaching, quiet results, campaign closure and accessibility before optional
character and review expansion. Human playtests and physical-device acceptance
remain open. These notes describe source work, not a new published package.

## Your journey, safely saved

Journey Map and Resume stay with the selected session. Solo and Hot Seat lesson
progress remain separate. Opening a map preserves the player, roster and turn.
An unrelated saved run no longer takes over the current journey.

Cloud saves now carry Journey queues, current positions, fan-level completion
and Mac L2 carry-over snapshots with their owning session. Cleared Journey queues
remain cleared when an older device reconnects. Incoming progress waits for a
safe menu, and Hot Seat handovers stay paused until Ready.

## Smoother sync

Credential access, encryption and cloud-state writes run in the background to
keep menus responsive. Credential merges preserve newly linked libraries.
Partial uploads and saves made during an upload remain pending until they reach
the linked libraries.

Tester Sync offers one-button setup for the shared tester library. Setup survives
relaunch and works offline. The page shows Syncing, Synced, Saved on this device
or Offline separately from the enabled state. The shared library includes all
players, solo progress and saved Hot Seats, including inactive sessions.

## Clearer navigation and controls

- Start an official level from its Play Insights row using mouse, keyboard or
  accessibility. Normal availability and campaign unlock rules still apply.
- Browse individual releases in About with a separate notes pane, larger
  selection targets and clearer headings and lists. The installed release's
  notes are available offline before the update feed is published.
- The Zoom action and Superzoom/skip counters fade with the gameplay menu in Classic,
  The Tribes and Chronicles.
- Amiga Versus shows its requirement for two simultaneously connected
  controllers before play.

## Compatibility and scope

macOS 12.3 or later, on Apple silicon and Intel. Classic and The Tribes retain
their Complete classifications. Chronicles and NeoLemmix remain Beta.
Existing engine, content and rescue-target verification limits remain in force.

Both devices need the updated archive reader to exchange Journey snapshots.
Mobile Journey playback remains Classic-only. Sequel steps and Mac L2
carry-over data are retained without switching into a solo session.
iPhone, iPad and Apple TV builds are separate from this Mac candidate.

This is a source candidate. Live cross-device propagation, physical-device
journeys and final signed-package acceptance remain to be verified.
