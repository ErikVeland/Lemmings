# Ultimate Lemmings 1.7.6 — More Verified Routes

Build: 60
Release base: v1.7.3

## Fan levels and learning

The evidence catalogue now contains 2,153 Classic fan wins and 322 NeoLemmix
wins. The learning journey has 292 lessons, including 136 fan levels from 80
packs. Difficulty scores use winning replay evidence. Unverified levels remain
unscored.

The expanded diagnostics distinguish source-engine compatibility gaps from
levels that simply lack a recorded win. Native wins do not establish full
physics parity with the original source engine.

## Lemmings 2 and Lemmings 3

Preserved coverage now includes 75 of 120 Lemmings 2 routes, with 27 carry-over
variants, and 41 of 90 Lemmings 3 routes. Further bounded searches and source
comparisons are recorded separately from winning routes. Both games remain
Preview: the full campaign and source-physics gates are still open.

The Lemmings 3 intro movie gallery now plays its verified DOS OPL2 Intro
recording. Movie pause, resume and close control the music. Music volume, mute
and Silent settings apply. Other movies have no assigned soundtrack yet.

## Included since the last public release

This update also includes the unpublished 1.7.4 and 1.7.5 improvements below.

## Music and spatial sound

Modern music now reaches Adaptive DJ's MOD decks, restoring centred percussion,
a gentler stereo spread, subtle reverb and EQ. Switching Modern and Faithful
preserves the track position. Music remains stereo.

Gameplay effects follow their position relative to the camera and zoom across
Classic, Lemmings 2 and Lemmings 3. Offscreen effects become quieter. Lemmings 2
uses independent spatial voices, and Lemmings 3 adds builder warning sounds.
This is camera-relative sound, without head tracking. Lemmings 2 has no mapped
exit cheer, and recorded replay audio remains mono.

With HD Effects enabled, Classic and Lemmings 2 nuke countdowns briefly sweep
the music into a hollow midrange. Restrained bass supports the original comic
explosions, with a limit to prevent buildup. Undo restores the mix. Lemmings 3
has no mass-nuke countdown.

## Home, Goal and help

With Modern controls, H centres the entrance and G centres the exit. In
Lemmings 2, G first selects Hang Glider when available; pressing it again with
Glider selected centres the exit. Home and End remain available. Use / for hints
and ? for controls. Controller stick clicks already provide the same camera
actions: L3 for the entrance and R3 for the exit.

## Fresh sessions and clearer selection

New solo and Hot Seat sessions start at the beginning of the chosen campaign or
playlist. Previous sessions, profile achievements and scores remain saved.
Continue retains its saved position. Hot Seat handovers stay paused until Ready,
and failed runs offer separate retry and next-player actions.

Fan levels use artwork that matches their terrain. Floating Down! now shows its
snow landscape. The HUD follows the active journey position. Journey selection
and level preparation include fixes since 1.7.3.

Gameplay settings offer None, Obvious and Modern selection effects across all
three games. Modern adds a crisp white outline and green halo. Pausing holds the
halo, and reduced-motion and reduced-flash settings keep it steady.

## Update

Existing players can use Check for Updates after publication. The full
soundtrack is included in `UltimateLemmings-1.7.6-build60.zip`, which also
supports a fresh installation. Lemmings 2 and Lemmings 3 remain Preview.
