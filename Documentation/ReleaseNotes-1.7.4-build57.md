# Ultimate Lemmings 1.7.4 — Selection Effects and More Verified Levels

Build: 57
Release base: v1.7.3

## Selection effects

Choose **Settings > Gameplay > Selection**:

- **None** removes the selection highlight for an old-school look.
- **Obvious** restores the previous game-pixel halo and overhead marker.
- **Modern** adds a white shimmering outline and a subtle green bloom around
  the sprite. The outline stays two physical display pixels wide through zoom,
  Retina scaling and CRT projection. The halo gently pulses every two seconds.

The choice applies immediately and is saved across Classic, Lemmings 2 and
Lemmings 3. The Original preset selects None. The Modern preset selects Modern.
Choosing an individual effect sets the preset to Custom.

Reduced motion and reduced flashes keep the selection effects steady. Reduced
flashes also limit the Modern effect to standard display brightness.

## More verified levels

Five more Classic fan levels have verified winning replays. The total is now
2,141. This update also corrects replay evidence and expands the verification
records for Lemmings 2 and Lemmings 3.

Lemmings 2 and Lemmings 3 remain in Preview. NeoLemmix remains in Beta.
