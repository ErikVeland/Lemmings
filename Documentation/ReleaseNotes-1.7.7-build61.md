# Ultimate Lemmings 1.7.7 — Pixels and Punch

## Original cursors and clearer selection

Gameplay settings now offer Original and Modern cursors across Classic,
Lemmings 2 and Lemmings 3. Original uses the supplied Amiga pixels and colours:
a dotted cross over terrain and a square reticule over a lemming. The Original
preset selects this cursor. Modern retains the current reticule. Manual choices
are saved as Custom.

Both 1× and 2× selected-skill icons are three times their previous size. The
optional lemming counter is half its previous size.

## Optional Classic skill backpacks

Assigned Climbers wear brown backpacks, Floaters wear orange backpacks, and
lemmings with both skills wear purple backpacks. The markers follow the PC and
Mac sprite artwork. Enable or disable Show skill backpacks in Gameplay settings.
Modern enables them by default; Original disables them.

## A deeper nuke countdown

With HD Effects enabled, the music gradually loses high frequencies and gains
bass from the start to the end of a nuke countdown. The mix stays more audible
instead of becoming quiet immediately. The sweep follows simulation time,
including pause and speed changes, and restores correctly on undo or recovery.
The original comic explosion and restrained bass impact remain.

This applies to Classic, imported NeoLemmix levels and Lemmings 2. Lemmings 3
has no mass-nuke countdown.

## Fan levels and verification

An exact bundled Golems challenge pack now selects its matching native mechanics
in gameplay and previews. Pass Interference has a recorded winning route using
ordinary player assignments. The evidence catalogue now records 2,154 Classic fan wins
and 322 NeoLemmix wins. Additional source-rule and sequel investigations are
retained separately from winning routes.

Lemmings 2 and Lemmings 3 remain Preview. NeoLemmix remains Beta. Recorded native
wins do not establish complete source-engine physics parity. Some Golems evidence
replays still differ in final state or completion timing.

## Update

For a fresh installation, choose `UltimateLemmings-1.7.7-build61-slim.zip`.
It includes the original soundtracks and offers the extra libraries on first
launch. Previously downloaded libraries are detected and retained.

Existing players can use Check for Updates. Its full archive preserves music
bundled inside older apps. Both downloads contain the same universal macOS app
and support macOS 12.3 or later.
