# Ultimate Lemmings 1.9 — The Tribes, Complete

Build: 75
Release base: v1.8.5

Lemmings 2: The Tribes is complete and leaves beta. Lemmings 3: Chronicles
moves from Preview to Beta. This update also restores more of the original
Macintosh presentation and improves saved runs, Hot Seat, music and updates.

## The Tribes is complete

All 120 levels have preserved winning routes. All twelve tribes have continuous
ten-level runs, with verified survivor carry-over. This release includes the
latest climbing, stone-collision and native runtime corrections.

## Chronicles moves to Beta

Lemmings 3: Chronicles now carries the Beta label throughout the library and
native game window. The retained evidence covers 74 of 90 standalone wins.
Work continues on the remaining routes, original-engine fidelity and endings.

## Original Macintosh presentation

Choose **Macintosh (original)** in Audio for 31 arrangements rendered with the
game's original sampled instruments: 21 Classic, six Oh No! and four Xmas tunes.
Release-rate changes use the original MousePress sound and changing pitch.
Choose **Original Macintosh** under Graphics → Counters for the original
skill-counter digits. These choices are independent of terrain artwork.

## Saved runs and Hot Seat

Resume shows every participating player's initials in turn order. Long rosters
wrap inside the button. Journey wins save at completion, before Next level.
A visible Menu action, Q and Escape return to the library with the saved run.
Classic, Lemmings 2 and Lemmings 3 share these controls.

## Music and update reliability

Music-source changes retain manual and interruption pauses. Journey and shuffled
music playback retain their continuity. The download icon and Check for Updates
share a recoverable flow after repeated clicks, cancellation or a busy update.

## Compatibility and validation scope

Universal macOS app for Apple silicon and Intel, macOS 12.3 or later.
NeoLemmix remains Beta. The slim download includes original Mac music and
essential soundtracks. Automatic updates retain the full soundtrack bundle.

The L2 recorded tribe chains finish with one survivor. The golden-talisman ark
ending and complete original-hardware fidelity remain separate validation.
L3 retains sixteen missing standalone routes and open campaign/ending checks.
Original-Mac listening comparison, physical Intel, minimum-macOS, controller
and VoiceOver listening journeys remain unverified.
