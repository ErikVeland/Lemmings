# Ultimate Lemmix 2.4.4

Build: 116
Release base: 6892e2f9
Platform builds: Mac 116, iPhone/iPad/Duo 113, Apple TV 112

Play 5,051 Classic fan levels across 487 packs and the 120 NeoLemmix Introduction levels.
The level data, sprites, objects and terrain are included for offline play.

If you hold a licence for the original Lemmings, download the game assets from Game Data. Ultimate Lemmix then uses the original artwork, music and game names.

Desk Clock can show your next timed calendar event. To enable it, open the Ultimate Lemmix menu and choose Show next event in Desk Clock widget….
