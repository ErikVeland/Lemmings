# 1.9.2 build 77 distribution evidence

Release: [Ultimate Lemmings 1.9.2, build 77](https://github.com/ErikVeland/Lemmings/releases/tag/v1.9.2).
Frozen source: `71559011ce09631479bc27644abe60b348bd88ae`. Release base: `v1.9.1`. Tag: `v1.9.2`.
Published: 2026-10-11T14:00:50Z.

This maintenance release restores completion music and a subtle golden rim,
credits precision rewards in learning and Hot Seat journeys, moves Cellbash to
lesson 2 and Come back! to Intermediate, fades the gameplay Menu button, restores
Mac menu-bar access, fixes Records campaign navigation and adds transient song
and soundtrack notices. Shared artwork, settings help and compatible action
replays are included. iOS 2.0 remains separate development work.
