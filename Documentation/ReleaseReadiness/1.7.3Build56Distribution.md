## Optional archives not published

The slim archive built and signed, but Apple then reported that the
`lemmings-beta` keychain credential profile was missing. The same profile had
successfully notarised the standard archive earlier in this run. Explicit login
keychain lookup and credential-history retries also failed. The profile must be
restored before submitting additional archives. No credentials were extracted.

The publisher permits release without the optional slim archive. Only the fully
notarised standard archive was published. The slim submission and interrupted
Monterey/Game Center build outputs remain under
`.build/notarised/1.7.3-56-20260930-163822` for resuming. Additional builds were
stopped after the public release completed. This release record does not claim
successful notarisation or distribution of those optional targets.
