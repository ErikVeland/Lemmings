# Ultimate Lemmings 1.8 — Pixels and Sound

Build: 64

Release base: v1.7.9

[Download the slim app](https://github.com/ErikVeland/Lemmings/releases/download/v1.8/UltimateLemmings-1.8-build64-slim.zip)

## Sharper pixels

Pixel artwork keeps crisp edges when scaled across game views, controls,
level previews, hints and original movies.

## Sound fixes

NeoLemmix now plays hatch, assignment, rescue, death and nuke sound effects
through the selected Classic sound bank. Custom gadget samples, builder
warnings and steel-contact effects remain incomplete.

After the final nuke explosion, slowed music smoothly returns to normal speed
in Classic, NeoLemmix and Lemmings 2, including failed results. The visual
failure mood remains. Rewinding into the countdown restores the effect.
Lemmings 3 has no mass-nuke control.

## Lemmings 3 correction

Brick pickups provide eight uses, matching the original game. Lemmings 2 and
Lemmings 3 remain Preview. Their full completion milestone moves to 1.9.
NeoLemmix remains Beta. Mobile remains the separate 2.0 milestone.

## Download and update

Choose the slim download for a fresh installation. Optional soundtrack
libraries remain available in the app. Check for Updates uses the full archive
to preserve bundled music. Both downloads support Intel and Apple silicon Macs
running macOS 12.3 or later.
