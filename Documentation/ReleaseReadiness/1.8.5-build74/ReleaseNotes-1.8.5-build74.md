# Ultimate Lemmings 1.8.5 — Back to the Lemmings

Build: 74
Release commit: ecafd64c09ca0f87e3ad45d842683ab9b2a4b11d
Release base: v1.8.4

Keep favourite levels close and return to the ones you have played recently.

## Favourites

Bookmark a level from Level Select or its result screen. The outlined star fills
when saved. Open Collections from the home screen or pack browser to find it again.
Remove a favourite with the same control.

## Recently Played

Collections keeps the last 50 distinct levels, with the most recent first.
Playing a level again moves it to the front. Start opens a fresh attempt;
the existing Resume action restores a saved attempt.

## Personal collections across the library

Each player has separate favourites and history. Hot Seat records a visit for
the player who owns the attempt, and result bookmarks belong to that player.
Collections cover Classic campaigns, fan packs, NeoLemmix packs, Lemmings 2 and
Lemmings 3. Locked, missing and changed entries stay visible but cannot start.
Favourites and history survive relaunch, with validated backup recovery and
protection against overwriting another app's save.

## Compatibility

Universal macOS app for Apple silicon and Intel, macOS 12.3 or later.
NeoLemmix remains Beta. Lemmings 2 and Lemmings 3 remain Preview.
The slim download includes essential music and offers optional soundtrack libraries.
Automatic updates retain the full soundtrack bundle.
