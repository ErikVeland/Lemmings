## Scope and limits

The user instructed publication when notarised, then explicitly granted permission
for the remaining foreground and audible checks. The published signed app passed
its 15-second empty-home launch smoke test: first frame 4.106 seconds, with music
starting afterward. The foreground controls-help and pause-menu hint handoff
passed with audio muted. Both ran through `Tools/UITestRunner/run.py`.

The offscreen app checks passed, including movie controls, welcome
rendering and Continue, all 292 journey entries and Solo/Hot Seat across Classic,
L2 and L3. The core audit passed 48 checks with no source drift.

Preserved sequel evidence covers 75 L2 routes, 27 L2 carry-over variants and 41
L3 routes. The full sequel campaign and source-physics gates remain open. Both
games retain Preview status. NeoLemmix remains Beta. Native fan wins and
object-rule comparisons are not claims of full source-physics parity.

Physical Intel/minimum-macOS tests and an installed Sparkle upgrade remain
unverified. One structural-evidence verifier lacked its ignored descriptor file;
its earlier record is not newly verified. Optional slim, Monterey and Game Center
archives are not published for this build.
