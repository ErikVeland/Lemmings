## Explosion positioning

Classic/NeoLemmix, Lemmings 2 and Lemmings 3 retain each explosion's world
position and project it through the current camera on every effect frame.
Camera pan, zoom, window resize, CRT curvature and precision lens transforms
therefore move the effect with the terrain. Effect age and exposure do not
restart when the camera changes. Short local flashes use the same projection.

## Solution replay sound effects

Solution playback now sends each simulated tick's positioned cues to a separate
sound player. It copies the selected sample bank, volume, mute and bottom-fall
settings without copying the live attempt's voices or movie-recording callback.
Volume and mute changes also reach an open solution replay. Sound positions
follow the replay camera. Pause and dismissal silence playback; rewind rebuilds
silently, while forward single-step plays only the new tick. Fast-forward emits
each tick's cues. Recorded movie audio and L2/L3 playback are unchanged.

The offscreen, muted `solution-audio` regression passes with bundled Fun 1:
entrance, assignment and exit cues; bank/settings inheritance; matching cue
sequences at normal and fast speed; silent pause and rewind; single-step cues;
and unchanged live simulation. No audible test was run. The log is
`.build/solution-audio-tests.log`.
