## Beast rhythm balance and rewind sound

Modern mode now centres the supplied Beast I bank's bass, bass drum, snare
and tom. The `b1bsdr` sample name was not recognised as percussion. The fix
matches the title and instrument names, leaves melodic instruments alone,
and keeps Faithful panning unchanged. The bass drum also participates in
rhythm-only playback. An offline test isolates the bank's actual bass waveform
and checks Modern stereo balance against Faithful's original separation.

Rewind voices previously fed their rendered samples back into the recent-effect
buffer. Repeated scrubs could therefore replay and amplify earlier scrubs.
Rewind voices are now excluded from that buffer. Playback retains reversed
sample order, clamps the captured mix, caps scrub voices at four, and shares
one gain budget between active scrub voices. This shared SoundEffectPlayer path
covers Classic, NeoLemmix, Lemmings 2 and Lemmings 3.

The offline regression passes 1,200 scrub requests with exact reverse ordering,
a four-voice limit, bounded summed amplitude and unchanged source history.
Spatial output, camera movement, mute, pause backlog and the L2 shared audio
graph checks also pass. Evidence: `.build/beast-mix-tests.log`,
`.build/rewind-spatial-tests.log` and `.build/rewind-final-tests.log`.
All tests were muted or rendered offline. Audible verification remains manual.
These changes are pending the next local build.
