# 1.9.1 build 76 distribution evidence

Release: [Ultimate Lemmings 1.9.1, build 76](https://github.com/ErikVeland/Lemmings/releases/tag/v1.9.1).
Frozen source: `9ba3fc25c42a57cd89394680d46e5aee79ba3d44`. Release base: `v1.9`. Tag: `v1.9.1`.

This hotfix groups the targeting preferences, disables tube controls for Flat
Panel, favours wall-facing targets in crowds and starts nuke funeral music at
the 2→1 countdown. Saturation still follows the point where rescue is impossible.
Classic, NeoLemmix and both sequels share targeting. Lemmings 3 has no mass nuke.
The 1.9 release classifications and campaign limits remain unchanged.

## Validation

The [minor audit](1.9.1-build76/audit/report.md) passed all 48 checks with zero
source drift. The [release-tool suite](1.9.1-build76/release-tools.log) passed all
36 tests. The five broader audit groups remain not run: replay movies, complete
app integration, performance measurement, sequel views and arcade records.

Focused checks from this session cover:

- [Settings](1.9.1-build76/hotfix/settings-ui-runtime.log): pixel grouping,
  checkbox targets, keyboard input, Flat Panel disabled controls and re-enabling
  saved values for Monitor and Television.
- [Classic targeting](1.9.1-build76/hotfix/approach-classic-build.log): nearby-wall
  direction, a real Basher assignment and terrain cut, plus a hovered target
  that turns before the click. [L2](1.9.1-build76/hotfix/approach-l2.log) and
  [L3](1.9.1-build76/hotfix/approach-l3.log) cover their native selection and
  priority rules. L3 has no Basher slot.
- [Countdown transitions](1.9.1-build76/hotfix/nuke-transition.log): 15/17-tick
  one-count boundaries, separate saturation, staggered explosions, recovery,
  rewind, undo and ordinary loss. Native [Classic/Neo](1.9.1-build76/hotfix/nuke-classic-neo-runtime.log)
  and [L2](1.9.1-build76/hotfix/nuke-l2-runtime.log) checks cover HD on and off,
  final-pop recovery and the stopped result screen.
- [Offline audio DSP](1.9.1-build76/hotfix/nuke-dsp-runtime.log): spectral sweep,
  voice limits, mute and the 2.4-second return after the final audible pop.
- [What's New](1.9.1-build76/release-notes-runtime.log): current release labels,
  scroll bounds at three sizes, visible Continue targets and keyboard input.
