arm64/x86_64 code on macOS 12.3 or later. The full update retains bundled music;
the slim download carries essential music and optional soundtrack libraries.

app and shared core contain arm64 and x86_64 slices, targeting macOS 12.3.

## Limits

All retained native UI checks stayed offscreen and test audio stayed muted.
No audible listening, foreground shipping-app launch, physical Intel or
minimum-macOS test, installed Sparkle upgrade, sustained performance benchmark
or complete sequel campaign certification was performed by this audio pass.
Listening is still needed to judge perceived balance, loop joins and the
largest rescue chorus.

Paused-win music resume in the native L2/L3 windows was source-reviewed and
compiled. Runtime completion-music assertions cover Classic and fan-pack flows;
they do not validate native sequel completion music. The focused completion,
Ready and replay scope passed separately. The broader Hot Seat preflight stopped
with `The all-level run did not use its player-facing name and journey progress`,
so that run does not establish full Hot Seat coverage.

The new sequel supplements do not establish the identities, timing or mix of
anonymous original environmental samples. These limits retain the Beta and
Preview labels. The 1.8.4 What's New page passed offscreen rendering at 900×620, 1120×720
and 1600×1000. Scrolling, clipping, mouse hit targets and keyboard Continue
passed. The renders were also inspected. [Log](1.8.4-build73/release-notes.log)
and [renders](1.8.4-build73/release-notes-layout/) are retained.
