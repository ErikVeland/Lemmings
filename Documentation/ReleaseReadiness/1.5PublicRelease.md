outside this source-only pass.

## Release scope

The release targets the completed 352-level Classic campaign. Lemmings 2 and
Lemmings 3 remain Preview. NeoLemmix remains Beta or Preview. The 1.5 notes
list fixes since build 38 and separate fixes already shipped in builds 40–41
from new candidate changes. Do not claim every crash is fixed until the fresh
packaged app and update tests pass.
