## Balanced cursor badges

The selected skill icon, empty-skill red X and optional lemming counter now
match in visible height and alignment at both 1× and 2×. The X uses the
game's pixel font. Transparent sprite padding no longer makes an icon look
smaller. The shared sizing applies to Classic, Lemmings 2 and Lemmings 3.

## A sharper nuke finish

With HD Effects enabled, the music filter opens back up over 280 ms after
the final nuke pop. The bass lift returns to normal with it. The transition
continues through explosion tails and the results screen, even after the
simulation stops. Classic and Lemmings 2 follow their own audible pop timing.
Imported NeoLemmix levels also restore the mix. Lemmings 3 has no mass nuke.

## Fan levels and the learning path

The evidence ledger now records 2,305 Classic fan wins, up from 2,154 in
1.7.7, alongside 322 NeoLemmix wins. Exact-level rule selection, Golems hatch
placement and directional mining compatibility improve. The regenerated
292-lesson learning path draws on the expanded set of recorded solutions.

Lemmings 3 includes a focused directional step-up correction supported by
source investigation and retained-route checks. Lemmings 2 and Lemmings 3
remain Preview. NeoLemmix remains Beta. Native winning replays do not prove
complete source-engine physics parity, and source outcome differences remain.
