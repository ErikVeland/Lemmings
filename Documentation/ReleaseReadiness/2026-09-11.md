## Delivered behaviour

- Switching apps or losing game-window focus pauses active gameplay at normal speed. Resume is explicit.
- Disconnecting a controller that has been used pauses the level. Disconnecting an unused controller does not interrupt keyboard play.
- Hint, help and replay close handlers preserve interruption pauses.
- Gameplay settings can disable automatic pause. The preference persists and follows modern/OG presets.
- Controller settings navigation skips unavailable choices in either direction.
- The complete default app integration run includes the recent QoL checks. Screenshot output directories are created by the tests.

## Limits

Rosetta is not physical Intel testing. The host does not have an iOS SDK. No iOS, Switch, PlayStation or Xbox application was built. Physical controllers, minimum-OS hardware, sustained performance, accessibility, remaining campaign coverage and publishing arrangements remain open in [the gate register](gates.json).

The earlier notarised beta archive is separate. The updated app is at `.build/local/Ultimate Lemmings.app`; this pass did not create a new public-release archive. A later candidate must be frozen, signed and notarised with its own verification record.
