# Ultimate Lemmings 2.2.2

Draft for the next release. Verify each claim against the frozen candidate before
publication. Build numbers and the release commit are assigned at the source cut.

## Made for Duo. Refined everywhere.

Duo has driven a wider round of improvements to Ultimate Lemmings. Version 2.2.2
gives its changing displays more care, while improving the shared experience on
Mac, iPhone, iPad and Apple TV.

## More room on Duo

Menus and gameplay use the full front display, with controls arranged around
camera cutouts. Layouts adapt between the outer screen and divided inner display.

Desk Clock opens reliably from Home and fills the active display. Tent Pose can
open it during a game, preserving the attempt and pausing play until you return.
The clock follows your display brightness, offers optional red night vision and
improves how its lemmings move around the digits and reach their doorway.

## A clearer route through the game

Shared navigation gives menus a consistent route home and keeps Back controls
within reach on long pages. Cancelling an action returns to the page you came
from. Hot Seat handovers still wait for Ready.

The fan library groups learning levels, magazines and books, community
collections, ports and remakes. Numbered packs stay together in order. Your
progress, favourites and saved runs keep their existing identities.

Winning a Golden Path lesson also credits the level in its original collection.
Existing saved wins receive the same credit without another playthrough.

## Familiar stone, clearer controls

Shared buttons use rock artwork drawn from the original Macintosh game.
Gameplay controls have distinct faces, readable symbols and consistent states
across Mac, iPhone, iPad and Apple TV.

Touch controls make overlapping lemmings easier to browse. Revised mobile replay
controls keep the title compact, skills visible and camera movement within reach.
Crowded playfields gain subtle sprite separation at closer zoom levels.

## Platforms and scope

The release covers Mac on Apple silicon and Intel, iPhone, iPad, Duo and Apple TV,
with the existing TestFlight and AltStore Classic channels. Apple processing and
Beta Review determine external TestFlight availability.

Classic and The Tribes remain Complete. Chronicles and NeoLemmix remain Beta
with their documented limits. Mobile Journey still plays Classic levels.
The release record will distinguish automated checks from physical-device
acceptance and record delivery status for each platform.
