# Ultimate Lemmings 2.6

Build: 111
Release base: v2.5
Platform builds: Mac 111, iPhone/iPad/Duo 48, Apple TV 23

## Home, Play and Settings

Start or resume from the large Home button. Play groups the games above other saved routes and Collections. Game names stay on one line. Settings groups related controls and keeps navigation close to the active pane.

## Gameplay and replays

The gameplay and replay controls keep speed, pause and rewind within reach. Solution replays have a timeline with marks for successful skill assignments. Scrub to a position or jump between moves. Faster playback reuses movie frames to reduce capture overhead.

## Music

Adaptive DJ uses soundtrack variants in a set order for campaign routes and follows the active level in fan queues.

## Compatibility

macOS 12.3 or later on Apple silicon and Intel. Classic and The Tribes are Complete. Chronicles and NeoLemmix remain Beta.
