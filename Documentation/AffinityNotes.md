# Affinity notes

The popovers separate a philosopher's ideas from the game's classification of a
run. The classification is based on recorded rescues, skills and interventions.
The fictional affinities are labelled as fictional.

Mill's summary covers utilitarianism and differences in the quality of pleasures.
[Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Mill](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/mill/).

Kant's summary covers moral duty and respect for persons.
[Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Kant's moral philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/kant-moral/).

Singer's summary covers reducing suffering and directing resources effectively.
[Peter Singer: About](https://www.petersinger.info/about).

Aristotle's summary covers practical judgement and virtue. Epicurus's summary
covers freedom from pain and anxiety.
[Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Ancient ethical theory](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/ethics-ancient/).

Hobbes's summary covers peace, security and political authority.
[Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Hobbes](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/spr2024/entries/hobbes-moral/).

Sartre's summary covers freedom and responsibility.
[Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Sartre](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/sartre/).

Popper's summary covers falsifiability and revision in response to evidence.
[Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Popper](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/spr2021/entries/popper/).
