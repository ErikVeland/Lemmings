# Ultimate Lemmings 2.4.1

Build: 105
Release base: a19e9ad44681fe286f8a69c32d522295514ab459
Platform builds: Mac 105, iPhone/iPad/Duo 44, Apple TV 19

## Pick the worker

Crowd targeting favours eligible lemmings using a skill, including a Digger you want to turn into a Basher. Their priority remains after the skill ends, until they fall. Your existing targeting preference stays in place. Held touch selections and controller locks keep the individual you chose.

This applies to Classic, The Tribes, Chronicles and supported NeoLemmix content.

## Save and close

Mac saves locally before waiting for cloud delivery at quit. The cloud wait is limited to three seconds. Interrupted sync remains pending for a later retry. Cancelled cloud work cannot report a new successful sync.

## Accessibility

Fix the Mac Home crash when Magnet or another accessibility tool queries the window. Footer actions now have matching labels and destinations, including Leaderboards, Playlists and Donate.

## Home loading

Home reuses bundled Tribes level data when refreshing completion counts. Player progress stays current, and imported levels retain fresh content checks.

## Compatibility

macOS 12.3 or later, on Apple silicon and Intel. Classic and The Tribes are Complete. Chronicles and NeoLemmix remain Beta. The mobile Journey is Classic-only.
