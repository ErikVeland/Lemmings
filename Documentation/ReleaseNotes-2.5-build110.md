# Ultimate Lemmings 2.5

Build: 110
Release base: 40b6009004055fac86611e88540e82ebad3edb26
Platform builds: Mac 110, iPhone/iPad/Duo 48, Apple TV 23

2.5 brings the UX overhaul described in [UX redesign](UXRedesign.md).

## Home and Play

Home offers one next step. Play holds every game and collection. The Mac menu bar brings Play, Profile and Community within reach from other screens.

## Speed and rewind

Choose a speed directly. Rewind counts back in game seconds.

## Results and records

Results show how many lemmings you rescued and the stars you earned. Open Details for the level leaderboard, records and replay options. Profile groups achievements by category. The result screen lowers and muffles the level music until you leave.

## Compatibility

macOS 12.3 or later, on Apple silicon and Intel. Classic and The Tribes are Complete.
Chronicles and NeoLemmix remain Beta.
